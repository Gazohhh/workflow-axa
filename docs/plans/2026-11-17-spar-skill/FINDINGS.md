### Reference skills studied (read-only clones in the session scratchpad, 2026-11-17)

### Claude's independent position (v3), sealed before the GPT design was requested

- `/spar` stays one small user-invoked skill (D1, D3) plus one reference file for the brief and the objection ledger; it composes, never re-implements: discovery through workflow-clarify step 1 inside a task, `codebase-design` for architecture weakness, `domain-modeling` for terms, optional `research` and `diagnosing-bugs` when installed, `grilling` for every owner question.
- Frame first (from arena): the decision in one sentence, the rubric (3-6 criteria a good answer must meet), whether a codebase or task is involved, the depth. Without a codebase or task: chat-only, no records.
- Foundations: write down the premises the existing code, docs, tests and decisions rest on, test the ones the decision depends on (attack-the-premise), mark each checked / contradicted / unverified, and say whether a refactor must come first. Proportional: only what the decision touches.
- Independence: round 0 is blind. Each model writes its own position from the same frame and facts; Claude's is saved before GPT's arrives; neither sees the other's yet. Then cross-examination rounds over an objection ledger (stable IDs; open, resolved, needs-owner, unverifiable). A concession needs a reason or evidence; "agree" without one doesn't close an objection. The proposal under debate may switch to GPT's when it scores better on the rubric.
- Depth: quick 1, standard 3, deep 5, N, until-agreement; default from one setting, overridable per invocation.
- Stopping (until-agreement has no round cap): progress = a round that resolves or opens an objection with new evidence, or changes the proposal; stalled = a round without progress; an objection re-raised without new evidence counts as repeated; an objection that needs unavailable evidence becomes unverifiable and goes to the owner as an assumption or question; open owner decisions pause the debate; the owner cancels in plain words any time; one progress line per round.
- Output: the agreement fields the owner listed, plus who the opponent was and whether the debate was cross-model; inside a task it becomes the clarify agreement's Approach (WHAT and WHY) and `/workflow` takes over for HOW; drop v2's "debate implementation choices on an agreed task" (HOW belongs to the workflow).
- Fallback unchanged (D2), labelled "single-model sparring, not cross-model" in every round and the result.
