- I2 Protocols looked at: AI safety via debate (Irving, Christiano, Amodei 2018; arxiv.org/abs/1805.00899): two debaters, a human judge decides; later versions narrow each round to one contested claim. Delphi: a facilitator gives controlled feedback between rounds; most movement happens in rounds 1 and 2. RAND/UCLA: a moderated discussion between rating rounds. All three have a person who judges or feeds back between rounds.
