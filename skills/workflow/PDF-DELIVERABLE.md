# Requested reader-focused PDF

Load only when a PDF is requested. This adds document work to the core workflow; it is not a second workflow or a developer handoff exported unchanged. Keep agent records separate from the reader's document. Include the PDF, editable source and review work in the approved scope and TODO, even when the associated code edit is small.

## 1. Establish the reader and reuse approved language

Create `PDF-BRIEF.md` inside the task folder. Record the audience, purpose, document language, scope, source material, and a map for product concepts that need careful wording:

| Concept | Verified meaning/source | Reader wording and immediate explanation | Approval reference/status |
|---|---|---|---|

Read any existing owner-approved audience brief or product glossary first. Reuse it when the audience, purpose, language and meaning are unchanged; record its location/version and why it applies. Do not require another confirmation for unchanged approved terms or ordinary connective language. Approval of terminology establishes a language choice, not the truth of a factual claim.

Ask about missing audience/purpose/language information and genuinely new, disputed or meaning-changing mappings. Present these with the development plan when possible. A user's "approve all recommendations" can confirm clearly presented document choices as well as the plan; an ambiguous "go" does not select unresolved alternatives. Persist the approval or applicable earlier approval before drafting dependent prose, captions or diagram labels. Independent source investigation and already approved implementation can continue.

**Ready to draft:** the reader brief and needed wording are confirmed or traceably covered by applicable existing approval. Unsupported meanings remain unknown, not silently simplified.

## 2. Track claims before writing

Maintain `PDF-EVIDENCE.md` with each substantive claim's source/location, category, relevant date/scope, and limitations. Distinguish verified facts, measurements, estimates, unknowns, proposals, approved decisions, and completed outcomes. Record contradictions rather than picking the convenient version.

Measurements need units and enough method, sample, period and limitations to interpret them correctly. Calculations retain inputs and method; estimates carry assumptions and appropriate uncertainty. A missing measurement is not zero. A planned benefit is not an observed improvement. Say who resolves an unknown and when only when those details are established.

A source conflict that changes meaning must be resolved or explicitly presented as unresolved. Every planned substantive claim needs support or an honest unknown/estimate label; terminology approval is not a substitute.

## 3. Write around the reader's need

Explain the situation, its consequence, the relevant decision/outcome and the next step. Choose an outline for that purpose rather than mirroring the repository or task log. Include the context the document needs to stand alone; do not rely on the chat or a glossary to make basic sentences understandable.

Use approved product language and natural, concrete sentences. Keep internal identifiers and technical detail in the working records unless the reader needs them to understand or act. Explain unavoidable technical concepts at first use without changing product meanings, roles, sequence or limitations. Remove repetitive conclusions, filler, inflated claims and decorative jargon. Make recommendations distinguishable from decisions.

Keep public source notes understandable and privacy-safe, with detailed traceability in the evidence record. Every retained detail should help the reader understand the situation, consequence, decision or next step.

## 4. Render and inspect

Use available PDF tooling and applicable host PDF-creation instructions. Keep the editable source and PDF in the approved task location. No separate skill installation is required; missing rendering capability is a documented blocker, not permission to install tools or claim a source file is a finished PDF.

Use readable typography, margins, spacing and heading hierarchy; apply supplied brand guidance, otherwise a neutral style. Keep text searchable/selectable. Use page numbers and navigation when useful. A simple diagram may clarify a process or decision, but must use confirmed wording and supported relationships. Do not replace a readable document with decorative cards or dense architecture charts.

Render and visually inspect every page at a readable size. Fix clipping, overflow, unreadable labels, bad page breaks, stranded headings, awkward table splits and footer overlap. Add space/pages rather than shrinking essential text. Extract text and compare it with the source to detect lost or corrupted content.

## 5. Review the exact final version

In `PDF-VALIDATION.md`, record the reviewed file/version, reviewer/method, evidence, verdict and findings for three distinct passes:

| Pass | Required review |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Compare substantive text, numbers, labels and diagram relationships with evidence and original sources, including units, status and uncertainty. |
| Reader comprehension | Review the PDF with only the approved audience/purpose. Identify missing context and explain the situation, consequence, decision and next step from the document itself. |
| Product meaning | Check the approved map and authoritative product sources; verify that wording preserves roles, distinctions, ordering and limitations. |

Use independent reviewers when available. Without them, perform all three as separate self-reviews and explicitly disclose that reader-perspective review is not an independent zero-knowledge test. Do not claim product expertise or a reviewer who was not present. If the user requires independent human/agent review, its absence remains a blocker unless explicitly waived.

Fix findings within approved wording and evidence. New meaning changes return for confirmation. After content revisions rerun all three passes on the updated document. For layout-only changes, rerender/inspect affected pages, verify extracted text, and record why any carried-forward content verdict still applies. Earlier-version success is not proof of the final file. If unresolved issues or unavailable tools stop review, report the partial deliverable and keep those gates open.

## 6. Deliver and record

Verify that approval references, source traceability, visual/text checks and all review verdicts identify the delivered version. Update TODO evidence and the outcome; unresolved required gates prevent `done`. Link the PDF and editable source, state its audience/purpose, and report actual review coverage and limitations. Keep the brief/evidence/validation records distinct from the reader deliverable. Local generation does not authorize publication or upload to an external service.
