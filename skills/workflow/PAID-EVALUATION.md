# Paid evaluation

Read this before you launch a separately paid model or evaluation batch: one that starts its own billed model sessions, such as a live host pilot or an evaluator run. [VISUAL-REVIEW.md](VISUAL-REVIEW.md) "Explicit exceptions and paid evaluation" sends you here. Normal authorized tool calls need no payment question.

Before **launching a separately paid model/evaluation batch**, present its cases, allowed sessions, subagents and retries, model and settings, and a hard total ceiling with units. Only the owner's actual cap, or a proposed-cap option they deliberately select, approves it; a suggested cap, `$[AMOUNT]`, "don't worry too much about costs", silence or an earlier batch's limit does not. The ceiling covers orchestration, child sessions, retries and the single retro; zero authorizes no paid calls. Bound in-flight spend with a provider limit or conservative reservations; if spend cannot be observed, disclose that and agree a measurable session or token limit first. Record budget, answer and usage in the existing evaluator record; a per-session subtotal is not the whole batch. Stop at the boundary or a safety violation, with no automatic retry or next batch. The package does not meter provider billing.
